Derailleur cheat sheet

Park Tool's method (Calvin Jones's videos), with Shimano's or SRAM's figure where it differs. Pictures drawn to scale, from where your eye goes. 17 October 2026.

Before any screw: hanger straight · chain not worn · cable runs freely · parts match. Adjusting cannot fix any of these.1,9

Rear

H · wire · index · L · B. Every limit: too tight, then back off till quiet.

1 H limit big ring · smallest cog

From behind · shifter fully released

Barrel adjuster in 2 turns, so the cable plays no part.1

H in half a turn at a time until the chain rubs the next cog in. Then out a quarter turn at a time until it is quiet. If two settings are equally quiet, take the tighter.1

Check by eye: pulley centre under the smallest cog's outer face.5,7

2 Wire

Shifter fully released · chain on the smallest cog

Barrel back out about 2 turns. Pull the cable taut, not hard, and clamp it.1

Shift through a few times; if slack appears, re-tension and re-clamp. Torques in the table at the end.

3 Index big ring · from the smallest cog

One click must move the chain exactly one cog. No move: barrel out a full turn. Two cogs: barrel in.1

Fine-tune: barrel out until the chain rattles on the next cog in, then in a quarter turn at a time until quiet. Check every cog, both ways.1

Shimano's check: on the 2nd cog, press the lever only through its free play. The chain should just touch the 3rd cog and rattle. Release: quiet.5

Unscrew to climb – barrel out moves the chain towards the bigger cogs.

4 L limit small ring · largest cog

Same trick: L in until the shift to the largest cog is slow or noisy, then out a quarter turn at a time until it shifts quickly and runs quiet.1

Check by eye: pulley centre under the largest cog's centre.5,7

Push the lever hard: the cage must not move further in. Too loose puts the chain in the spokes.1

5 B gap small ring · largest cog

5–6 mm, pulley teeth to cog teeth. Use a 5 or 6 mm hex key as the gauge.1,3 Tighten B = bigger gap.

Shimano's own sheets say only: as close as possible without touching, cranks turned backwards, checked on the smallest cog too.5 Park: if it shifts well, the gap is fine.1

A big B change moves the indexing: recheck step 3.1

6 Ride

Every gear, both ways, under real pedalling. A workstand does not load the drivetrain like the road.1

SRAM Rival 1 (Boardman)

SRAM's order is H, wire, L, then B with SRAM's chain-gap gauge on the largest cog (tallest teeth on the gauge's groove), and the barrel last.7 No millimetre figure. Barrel clicks: slow to a bigger cog, out; slow to a smaller cog, in.7

Front – 105 FD-5700-F on the Defy

Height and angle · L · wire · H · trim. Rear done first.

1 Height

From the drive side · cage pushed out by hand

Outer plate about 2 mm above the big ring's tallest tooth.

Park's video says 2–3 mm, Park's page 1–2 mm, Shimano 1–3 mm.2,4,6 Lower shifts better, if it clears.8

2 Angle

From above · chain on the big ring

Outer plate parallel with the big ring. Hold a hex key flat on it to see.2,6

Tail of the cage too far in: rotate the body anticlockwise; too far out: clockwise.2 Bolt: 5 N·m exactly on the carbon Defy until Giant gives a figure.6

3 L limit small ring · largest cog

From above · cable slack

Inner plate about 1 mm from the chain, as small as it goes without rubbing.2,4

Shimano allows 0–0.5 mm.6 Shift out a ring and back: a slow drop to the small ring means L out a quarter turn.2

4 Wire

Lever b clicked to the small-ring position

Barrel in, then out one turn. Pull the cable taut, clamp at 6–7 N·m. Shift a few times, take up any slack.6

The Defy's front cable needs a barrel: at the down-tube stop, or an inline SM-CA70 or SM-CA50.6

5 H limit big ring · smallest cog

From above · pressing the lever out

Start too loose, then in a quarter turn at a time until the outer plate is about 1 mm from the chain at its closest point in a crank turn.2

Shimano allows 0–0.5 mm.6 Slow shift up with full lever pressure: H out a quarter turn.2 Rub here under hard pedalling is frame flex: leave it.4

6 Trim, then ride big ring · largest cog

Press lever b to its light trim click only. Barrel until the inner plate is 0–0.5 mm from the chain.6

If trim drops the chain to the small ring: H out an eighth of a turn and repeat.6 Then shift both rings on several cogs, easing off the pedals.
